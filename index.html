ConnectionZ News

ConnectionZ is officially live! This version brings a more flexible, replayable puzzle experience with new ways to play, practice, and improve.

What’s New in v1.0Updated May 2026

ConnectionZ v1.0 is here! This release includes:

  • Three difficulty levels: Warm-Up, Brain-Bender, and Expert
  • Over 400,000 possible puzzle combinations through randomized boards
  • A Next Puzzle button to generate a fresh board
  • A Shuffle option to rearrange the cards
  • A Give Up button for when a puzzle gets too spicy
  • Updated scoring to make ranks feel more fair and achievable

Coming Next

We’ll keep adding new puzzles and categories, improving hints so they’re more useful, and reviewing player results to fine-tune the scoring system over time.
